# Prompts

Replace anything in [brackets].

## 1. Showroom photo cleanup

> Clean up this showroom photo of a [grey] [car model]. Remove fingerprints, dust, smudges and stray reflections of people or equipment from the paint and glass. Keep the exact car shape, panel gaps, wheels, badges, lights and interior unchanged. Keep the original camera angle, lens perspective and framing. Even, soft studio lighting. Photorealistic, high-end automotive catalogue quality, no added objects, no text.

## 2. Realistic paint reflections

> Enhance the paint of this [black] [car model] so it looks like a freshly detailed, deep gloss finish. Add soft, long, curved studio light reflections that follow the body lines along the doors, shoulder line and bonnet. Reflections must bend naturally with the curvature of each panel. Keep the colour exactly [black], no colour shift. Do not change the car's shape, wheels, badges or background. Photorealistic.

## 3. Background replacement

> Place this [white] [car model] in a [minimal dark concrete studio with a soft overhead light strip]. Keep the car exactly as in the original: same shape, angle, wheels, ride height and colour. Match the lighting direction and colour temperature on the car to the new background. Add a realistic contact shadow under the tyres and a subtle floor reflection. No floating, no distortion, no text, no extra cars.

## 4. Carbon fiber detail

> Enhance the exposed carbon fiber on the [front splitter / mirror caps / interior trim] of this [car model]. Show a clear, even 2x2 twill weave with a glossy clear-coat depth, sharp and consistent weave size across the whole part. Weave direction follows the part's shape. Do not change the part's shape or the surrounding paint. Macro automotive photography, photorealistic.

## 5. Golden hour exterior

> [Car model] in [grey], three-quarter front view, parked on a clean empty road at golden hour. Warm low sun from the left, long soft shadows, gentle rim light on the body edges, accurate reflections of the sky in the paint and glass. 35mm lens, eye-level camera, shallow depth of field. Correct proportions and wheel shape. Photorealistic, cinematic, luxury commercial style, no text, no logos added.

## 6. Video shot: drop and land (image-to-video)

> The [grey] [SUV model] drops from just above frame and lands on the road. On impact the suspension compresses realistically, the body dips and rebounds once, tyres flex slightly, a small puff of dust kicks out from the wheels. Camera is static, low angle, front three-quarter. Car shape, wheels and badges stay consistent in every frame. Realistic physics, cinematic grade, 24fps feel.

## 7. Video shot: slow orbit

> Slow, smooth camera orbit around the [car model] in [colour] from front three-quarter to side profile, in a dark studio with soft overhead light. Reflections move naturally across the paint as the camera moves. Car stays perfectly still and consistent, no warping of wheels or body. Cinematic, steady, luxury commercial.

## Checklist after each result

- Colour matches the real car (no shift)
- Finish looks like paint, not plastic
- Angle and lens perspective are believable
- Proportions, wheels and badges unchanged
- Lighting direction matches shadows
- Reflections follow the body curves
- No odd cuts, edges or melted details
