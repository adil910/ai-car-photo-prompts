# AI Car Photo Prompts

A prompt library for editing and generating automotive images with AI tools such as Higgsfield, Google Flow/Veo and Kling.

By **Adil Imtiaz** · [@by_adilvisuals](https://instagram.com/by_adilvisuals)

## Why

Luxury car images break easily with AI: paint turns plastic, reflections go wrong, wheels warp, badges melt. These prompts come from many rounds of fixing those problems on real dealership photos.

## Files

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `prompts.md` | Full prompts for showroom cleanup, paint reflections, background replacement, carbon fiber, and video shots |

## Rules I follow

- Always write the **complete** prompt, never a partial edit.
- Describe colours in simple terms ("grey exterior"), not manufacturer paint names.
- After each result, check colour, finish, angle, proportions, lighting, reflections and cuts, then fix the prompt.
- Lock what must not change (shape, wheels, badges, plates) in every prompt.
